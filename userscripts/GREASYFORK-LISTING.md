## Description (paste into the additional info / about box)

A small on-screen timer for GeoGuessr that tracks how long you take per round, with session
stats (rounds played, average, fastest and slowest time) and CSV export. It is built for
training your pace in Duels and NMPZ, where speed matters as much as accuracy.

The script runs fully in your browser. It makes no network requests, it never reads the
game's location data, and it does not reveal where you are, so it is a training aid rather
than a cheat. Round times are stored locally in your browser and can be exported to CSV.

Drag the panel to move it. Press Log at the end of each round to record the time and reset
for the next one. The timer also auto-resets when it detects a new round.

Open source under the MIT license.
